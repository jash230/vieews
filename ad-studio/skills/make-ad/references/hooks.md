# Hooks

The hook is the on-screen text (and spoken line, if any) in the first second or the first slide. Founders put most of their effort here. Pushcroll spent about 80% of its effort on the hook, and slide 1 decides about 70% of a slideshow's result (r/SideProject, 4.4M views). ([GTM Sauce hooks](https://gtmsauce.com/topics/video-hooks))

## Rules that held up across apps
1. **Open a loop and don't close it.** The Evolved Founder's question hook got ~50K views against 500 for a version that gave the answer away.
2. **Be specific and slightly provocative.** "Vague hooks flop." Good moves:
   - flip into a warning ("Don't buy this before reading")
   - hint at a secret ("The thing they won't tell you")
   - tell a relatable story ("My boss fired me and I'm glad he did")
3. **Name the right enemy or situation for the right audience.** Massive's "Stop using LinkedIn to apply to jobs" converted far better than an "Indeed" version that got 11M views, because it reached people who would pay.
4. **Sell the desire, not the feature.** Winners tapped attraction, status, self-improvement, entertainment and money. "Don't tell me what your app does, tell me how it will make my life better" (Blake Anderson: RizzGPT, Umax, Cal AI).
5. **The hook must lead to the app.** Clickbait that doesn't connect brings views and no downloads: Nomadtable got 300K views and 174 likes, and a Jenni AI debate video got 30M views but plugged the app too weakly.
6. **Keep it short:** about 12 words, two lines on screen. In the Gen Z style, emojis go on their own line (GlamUp: `never saw myself with the / right makeup routine / 😭😭`).
7. **Write like a person in the niche, not a brand.** Lowercase is fine for slideshows. No exclamation-mark ad voice.
8. **Never reuse a hook word for word.** `npm run pick` lists `usedHooks`. Also avoid near-copies (same sentence, one word swapped), unless you're deliberately varying a winner (`mode: "exploit"`). Then keep the winner's structure and change the situation.

## Writing three hooks
For the chosen pattern and angle, write three different hooks:
- one that's the plain, sharp version
- one with a more specific scenario (a number, a place, a person)
- one that leans into the tone (funny / dramatic / blunt…)

Pick the one a person in the audience would most likely stop for **and** that leads naturally to the app's magic moment.

## Captions
- First line = the hook (or a twist on it).
- Then one or two short lines that continue the story. Slideshows can finish the story in the caption (Knownwell).
- The kit's CTA is added automatically, plus 3–5 hashtags. Use niche hashtags, not #fyp spam. Hashtags don't affect reach much (Halo AI), so keep them few and relevant.
- No "link in bio" pleading, no all caps, no fake urgency.
